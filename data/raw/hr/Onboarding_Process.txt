ONBOARDING PROCESS - OPTIAGENT CONSULTING
==========================================

Welcome to OptiAgent Consulting! This document outlines your onboarding journey
for your first four weeks. Please read it before your start date.

DAY 1: ARRIVAL & ORIENTATION
-----------------------------
- Meet your direct manager and immediate team
- Complete the IT access request form at your workstation
- Receive your employee ID badge from Reception
- Tour of office facilities, meeting rooms, and emergency exits
- Introduction to company values, culture, and strategic goals

DAY 2: SYSTEM ACCESS & COMPLIANCE
-----------------------------------
- Set up workstation: laptop, email, Slack, Jira, and VPN
- Complete mandatory compliance training module (approximately 2 hours)
- Review and sign the Code of Conduct acknowledgement form
- Complete the data privacy and GDPR awareness module
- Set up multi-factor authentication (MFA) on all corporate accounts

DAY 3: TEAM INTEGRATION
------------------------
- Attend department stand-up meeting at 09:30
- Shadow a senior colleague for a half-day
- Review the current project portfolio and team objectives
- Set up a recurring 1-on-1 meeting with your line manager
- Complete the employee handbook acknowledgement form via the HR portal

WEEK 1: DEEP DIVE
-----------------
- Complete role-specific technical training (provided by your manager)
- Attend the company all-hands meeting if one is scheduled this week
- Complete your benefits enrollment (deadline: end of Day 5)
- Review key company policies: PTO, expenses, and business travel
- Begin the buddy programme with your assigned peer mentor

WEEK 2: RAMP UP
---------------
- Take on your first assigned project task or deliverable
- Complete any remaining compliance or role-specific modules
- Schedule introductory meetings with key internal stakeholders
- Review the performance review process with your manager
- Attend your first team social event (optional but encouraged)

WEEK 3-4: SETTLE IN
--------------------
- Become fully operational on your primary project
- Identify any skill gaps and discuss development plan with your manager
- Complete 30-day check-in with HR Business Partner
- Provide feedback on your onboarding experience via the HR portal survey
- Confirm your long-term hybrid working arrangement
