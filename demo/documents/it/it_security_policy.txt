IT SECURITY POLICY
OptiAgent Pty Ltd
Version: 2.1 | Effective Date: 1 February 2026 | Review Date: 1 February 2027
Owner: IT Security Team | Approved by: Chief Information Security Officer

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This policy establishes the minimum security standards all OptiAgent employees,
contractors, and third-party users must follow when accessing company systems,
networks, or data. Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
All account passwords must meet the following criteria:

  - Minimum length of 12 characters.
  - Must contain at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one
    numeric digit, and one special character (e.g. !, @, #, $).
  - Dictionary words, company names, usernames, or sequential patterns
    (e.g. "Password1!", "OptiAgent2026") are strictly prohibited.
  - Passwords expire every 90 days. Users will receive automated reminders
    at 14 days, 7 days, and 1 day before expiry.
  - The system enforces a password history of 10; the last 10 passwords may
    not be reused.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MFA is mandatory for all staff across all company systems, including Microsoft
365, VPN, cloud platforms, and internal applications. Microsoft Authenticator
is the preferred and supported MFA method. Hardware security keys (FIDO2) are
approved as an alternative for privileged accounts. SMS-based MFA is not
permitted for accounts with access to Confidential or Restricted data.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. DEVICE SECURITY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
All company-issued devices must have full-disk encryption enabled: BitLocker
on Windows and FileVault on macOS. Devices must be configured to lock
automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Screen lock requires password or
biometric authentication to resume.

Only company-issued laptops and desktops may be used to access production
systems or handle Confidential and Restricted data. Personal (BYOD) devices
are permitted for Microsoft 365 email and Teams only, provided they are
enrolled in the company Mobile Device Management (MDM) platform (Microsoft
Intune) and meet minimum OS patch requirements.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. SOFTWARE INSTALLATION AND ADMIN RIGHTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Standard users do not hold local administrator rights on company devices. All
software installations must be requested through the IT Helpdesk and approved
before installation. Unauthorised software, browser extensions, or plugins are
prohibited.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. REMOTE ACCESS AND VPN
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
All access to internal systems, shared drives, or on-premises resources from
outside the office network requires the use of the company VPN (Cisco
AnyConnect). Split-tunnelling is disabled by default. VPN credentials must not
be shared.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. DATA CLASSIFICATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OptiAgent classifies data into four tiers:

  Public      — Information approved for external release.
  Internal    — General business information not for public disclosure.
  Confidential — Client data, financial records, and project deliverables.
                 Requires encryption in transit and at rest.
  Restricted  — Highly sensitive data (PII, legal, credentials). Access
                 requires explicit approval; audit logging is mandatory.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. USB AND EXTERNAL MEDIA
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
USB storage devices and external media are prohibited on company devices unless
the device is an IT-approved, hardware-encrypted drive issued through the IT
Helpdesk. Unapproved USB devices are blocked at the endpoint level.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. PHISHING AWARENESS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected or suspicious emails.
Suspicious emails must be reported immediately using the Outlook "Report
Phishing" button. Do not forward or reply to suspected phishing messages.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10. SOCIAL MEDIA
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Employees must not disclose client names, project details, internal system
names, or any proprietary information on personal or professional social media
accounts without written approval from the Communications team.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
11. INCIDENT REPORTING
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
All suspected or confirmed security incidents — including lost devices, phishing
clicks, unauthorised access, or data exposure — must be reported to the Security
team at security@optiagent.com within 2 hours of discovery. Do not attempt to
investigate or remediate incidents independently.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
12. POLICY ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
All staff must acknowledge this policy annually. Failure to comply with any
provision may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
