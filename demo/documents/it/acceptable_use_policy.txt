ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY (AUP)
OptiAgent Pty Ltd
Version: 1.4 | Effective Date: 1 February 2026 | Review Date: 1 February 2027
Owner: IT & People & Culture | Approved by: Chief Operating Officer

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OptiAgent provides computing systems, network access, and digital tools to
support legitimate business activities. These resources are primarily for
business use. Limited, reasonable personal use is permitted provided it does
not interfere with work, consume significant bandwidth, or violate any
provision of this policy. This policy applies to all employees, contractors,
and any individual granted access to OptiAgent systems.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The following activities are strictly prohibited on any company system or
network:

  - Accessing, downloading, or distributing illegal, offensive, or
    discriminatory content.
  - Torrenting or peer-to-peer file sharing.
  - Cryptocurrency mining or any activity that consumes company computing
    resources for personal financial gain.
  - Gathering competitor intelligence through deceptive, unlawful, or
    unauthorised means.
  - Circumventing network security controls, firewalls, or content filters.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. INTERNET AND NETWORK MONITORING
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OptiAgent reserves the right to monitor internet usage, network traffic, and
device activity on company-owned systems and networks. Monitoring may occur
without prior notice and without individual consent beyond this policy
acknowledgement. Users should have no expectation of privacy when using
company systems.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. EMAIL AND COMMUNICATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Company email must be used in a professional manner. Emails representing
OptiAgent externally must maintain a courteous and professional tone. Personal
file transfers via company email are limited to attachments under 25 MB.
Company email must not be used for bulk personal correspondence or commercial
activities unrelated to OptiAgent business.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. CLOUD STORAGE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Approved cloud storage platforms are Microsoft OneDrive and SharePoint.
Company data must not be stored on personal cloud services including Google
Drive, Dropbox, iCloud, or similar platforms. Transferring company data to
unapproved services — even temporarily — constitutes a policy violation and
may also breach client confidentiality obligations.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. AI TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
External AI tools — including ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot (non-Microsoft 365
versions), and similar consumer-grade services — are prohibited for any work
involving Confidential or Restricted data, client deliverables, or internal
project material. OptiAgent is the company's approved and compliant AI
platform for all such use cases. Use of OptiAgent for business tasks is
encouraged; use of unapproved AI services for the same tasks is a disciplinary
matter.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. SOFTWARE LICENSING
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Employees may only use software for which OptiAgent holds a valid licence.
Installing unlicensed, cracked, or trial-expired software is prohibited. If
you require a software licence, submit a request through the IT Helpdesk.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. SYSTEM ACCESS AND CREDENTIALS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Employees must not share passwords, access tokens, or any form of
authentication credentials with colleagues or third parties. System access
must be appropriate to your job role; accessing systems, files, or data beyond
your authorised scope is prohibited, regardless of technical ability to do so.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. DATA BACKUP
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Employees are personally responsible for ensuring work-in-progress files are
saved to approved cloud locations (OneDrive or SharePoint) and not stored
solely on local drives. OptiAgent does not guarantee recovery of data stored
only on local device storage.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10. BRING YOUR OWN DEVICE (BYOD)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Personal smartphones may be used to access Microsoft 365 email and Microsoft
Teams provided the device is enrolled in the company's Intune MDM platform.
Personal laptops or desktop computers must not be used to access, store, or
process company data under any circumstances. Intune enrollment automatically
enforces minimum security baselines; employees consent to this by enrolling.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
11. CONSEQUENCES OF VIOLATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action proportionate to
the severity and intent of the breach, ranging from a formal warning through
to suspension, termination of employment, or referral to relevant authorities
where the conduct is unlawful.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
12. ANNUAL ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
All staff are required to read and formally acknowledge this policy each year.
Acknowledgement is completed via the HR portal. New employees must acknowledge
the AUP as part of onboarding before system access is granted.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
