1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

OptiAgent Solutions Ltd is a technology consulting firm headquartered in London, United Kingdom, with additional offices in Manchester, Edinburgh, and Dublin. Founded in 2014, we specialise in enterprise AI implementation, digital transformation, and intelligent process automation. Our 250 employees serve clients across financial services, healthcare, and public sector industries.

Our mission is to deliver measurable business value through pragmatic, responsible AI deployment. We are proud to foster a collaborative, inclusive, and high-performance culture that rewards both individual excellence and team contribution.

This handbook sets out the policies, benefits, and standards that govern employment at OptiAgent Solutions Ltd. All employees are required to read, understand, and comply with the contents of this document. Questions should be directed to the People & Culture team at hr@optiagent.co.uk.


2. ANNUAL LEAVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

All full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per leave year (1 January to 31 December). Leave accrues at a rate of 2.08 days per calendar month worked. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on contracted hours.

Employees must submit leave requests through the HR portal with a minimum of two weeks' notice for absences of three or more consecutive days. Shorter absences require at least 48 hours' notice where operationally possible. All requests are subject to manager approval.

Carry-Forward: A maximum of 5 unused days may be carried forward into the following leave year. Carried-forward days must be taken by 31 March or they will be forfeited. Carry-forward above the 5-day limit requires written approval from the Head of People & Culture and is granted only in exceptional circumstances.

Employees who leave the company will receive a payment in lieu for any accrued but untaken leave, or will be required to repay any leave taken in excess of their accrued entitlement.


3. SICK LEAVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Employees are entitled to 10 days of fully paid sick leave per leave year. Sick leave does not accrue and does not carry forward. Absences beyond 10 days in a leave year will be treated under the company's long-term illness policy and may be subject to statutory sick pay provisions.

Notification: Employees must inform their line manager by telephone (not solely by email or message) before the start of their contracted working hours on the first day of absence. Self-certification is accepted for absences of up to three consecutive working days.

Medical Certificate: A doctor's note (fit note) is required for any absence exceeding three consecutive working days. The fit note must be submitted to the People & Culture team within two working days of return. Failure to provide a fit note may result in the absence being recorded as unauthorised and unpaid.

Return-to-Work: A brief return-to-work discussion with the line manager is required after any absence, regardless of duration. This is a supportive measure to assist the employee's reintegration, not a disciplinary process.


4. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Employees are entitled to 12 public holidays per year in addition to their annual leave entitlement. The standard public holidays observed in England and Wales include, but are not limited to:

  - New Year's Day (1 January)
  - Good Friday
  - Easter Monday
  - Early May Bank Holiday (first Monday in May)
  - Spring Bank Holiday (last Monday in May)
  - Summer Bank Holiday (last Monday in August)
  - Christmas Day (25 December)
  - Boxing Day (26 December)

Employees based in Scotland or Ireland will follow the applicable regional public holiday schedule, which may differ. Employees required to work on a public holiday will receive a substitute day off to be taken within three months, subject to manager agreement.


5. PARENTAL LEAVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

OptiAgent Solutions Ltd is committed to supporting employees through major life events.

Primary Carer Leave: Employees who are the primary carer of a new child (by birth, adoption, or surrogacy) are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. This may be followed by additional statutory shared parental leave as permitted by law.

Secondary Carer Leave: Employees who are the secondary carer are entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid leave, which may be taken in one continuous period or as two separate blocks of 2 weeks each within the first year of the child's life or placement.

Eligibility: Employees must have completed their probationary period to qualify for enhanced parental leave pay. Employees must notify the People & Culture team at least 15 weeks before the expected date of birth or placement. Parental leave entitlement is separate from and in addition to annual leave.


6. WORKING HOURS AND FLEXITIME
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The standard working week at OptiAgent Solutions Ltd is 37.5 hours, typically spread across five days.

Core Hours: All employees are required to be available and working between 10:00 and 16:00 Monday to Friday (core hours). These are the hours during which meetings may be scheduled and collaboration is expected.

Flexitime Window: Outside of core hours, employees may choose to start as early as 07:00 and finish as late as 19:00 to complete their contracted hours. Flexitime arrangements must not compromise client commitments or team availability.

Overtime: OptiAgent Solutions Ltd does not routinely require employees to work beyond their contracted hours. Where overtime is agreed in advance with a line manager, time off in lieu (TOIL) will be offered at a rate of 1:1.


7. REMOTE WORK POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval and operational requirements. Remote working arrangements must be agreed in advance and reviewed on a quarterly basis.

Mandatory Office Days: All employees are required to attend the office on Tuesday and Thursday each week unless pre-approved leave or a client engagement necessitates otherwise. These anchor days support team collaboration, knowledge sharing, and culture building.

Remote Work Conditions: Employees working remotely must maintain a professional and secure working environment, use company-approved VPN access, and be available during core hours. Expenses for home office equipment may be claimed in line with the company's expenses policy.


8. PROBATIONARY PERIOD
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

All new employees are subject to a 3-month probationary period commencing on their start date. During probation, performance and conduct are reviewed informally on a monthly basis by the line manager.

At the end of the probationary period, the line manager will confirm in writing whether probation has been passed, extended, or whether employment will not be continued. Probation may be extended by written notice for a further period of up to 3 months where performance or conduct warrants it. Extension requires the approval of the Head of People & Culture.

During the probationary period, the notice period on either side is one week.


9. NOTICE PERIODS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Notice periods following successful completion of probation are as follows:

  - Less than 2 years of service:    1 month's notice
  - 2 to 5 years of service:         2 months' notice
  - 5 or more years of service:      3 months' notice

These periods apply to both employee resignations and company-initiated terminations (except in cases of gross misconduct, where summary dismissal without notice may apply). Payment in lieu of notice may be made at the company's discretion.


10. CODE OF CONDUCT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

All employees are expected to conduct themselves with professionalism, integrity, and respect at all times.

Confidentiality: Employees must not disclose confidential information relating to OptiAgent Solutions Ltd, its clients, or its business activities to any third party, during or after employment. All employees sign a confidentiality agreement as part of their contract of employment.

Conflicts of Interest: Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest to their line manager and the People & Culture team. This includes outside employment, business interests, or personal relationships that may influence professional judgement. The company will assess each case and determine any action required.

Dress Code: OptiAgent Solutions Ltd operates a business casual dress code. Employees are expected to dress professionally and appropriately for their role, their environment, and client interactions. On client-facing days or formal business occasions, smart business attire is required. Employees working remotely should dress appropriately when on video calls.


11. DISCIPLINARY PROCESS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

OptiAgent Solutions Ltd follows a fair and transparent disciplinary process in line with the ACAS Code of Practice.

  Step 1 — Verbal Warning: Issued for minor misconduct or performance concerns. Noted on the employee's HR record for a period of 6 months.
  Step 2 — Written Warning: Issued where a verbal warning has not resulted in improvement, or where misconduct is more serious. Valid for 12 months.
  Step 3 — Final Written Warning: Issued where previous warnings have not been heeded, or where conduct is sufficiently serious. Valid for 12 months.
  Step 4 — Dismissal: Where no improvement has been made following a final warning, or where gross misconduct has occurred, employment may be terminated.

At each stage, the employee has the right to be accompanied by a colleague or trade union representative, and the right to appeal any decision within 5 working days.
