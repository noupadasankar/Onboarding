OPTIAGENT SOLUTIONS LTD
EMPLOYEE BENEFITS GUIDE
Version 2.1 | Effective Date: 1 January 2026

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This guide summarises the benefits available to all permanent employees of OptiAgent Solutions Ltd. Benefits take effect from the first day of employment unless otherwise stated. For full scheme rules or to make changes to your selections, log in to the employee benefits portal at benefits.optiagent.co.uk or contact the People & Culture team.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. HEALTH INSURANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

All employees are enrolled in the BUPA Comprehensive Health Plan on their first day of employment. The plan covers the employee plus up to two dependents (spouse/partner and one child, or two children). There is no employee contribution — OptiAgent Solutions Ltd pays 100% of the premium.

Coverage includes: private in-patient and day-patient treatment, out-patient consultations and diagnostics, mental health support, cancer care, and access to BUPA's digital GP service (available 24/7 via app).

Dental Cover: Dental treatment is included within the BUPA health plan. Employees may claim up to £500 per year for routine and restorative dental treatment at any registered dentist. Orthodontic treatment for dependents under 18 is covered up to £250 per year.

Employees wishing to upgrade to family cover (more than two dependents) may do so at a subsidised rate. Contact the People & Culture team for a quote.


2. PENSION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

OptiAgent Solutions Ltd provides pension contributions through the NEST (National Employment Savings Trust) scheme. Employees contribute 5% of qualifying earnings, and the company contributes 8% — one of the most competitive employer pension contributions in the technology consulting sector.

Employees are auto-enrolled into the pension scheme after three months of employment. You may opt out by contacting NEST directly, though this is strongly discouraged. Employees may also elect to increase their own contribution above 5% via salary sacrifice, which provides an additional National Insurance saving.

Pension statements are issued annually and are accessible at any time via the NEST online portal.


3. LIFE INSURANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

All employees are covered under the company's group life insurance (death-in-service) policy. In the event of an employee's death while employed by OptiAgent Solutions Ltd, a lump sum of 4 times the employee's annual gross salary will be paid to the nominated beneficiary. Employees should ensure their beneficiary nomination is up to date via the HR portal.


4. EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE PROGRAMME (EAP)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

We recognise that employees may face personal challenges that affect their wellbeing and performance. The Employee Assistance Programme provides free, confidential support, including:

  - Up to 8 sessions of professional counselling (face-to-face, telephone, or video)
  - 24/7 telephone helpline staffed by qualified counsellors
  - Legal and financial guidance
  - Wellbeing resources and self-help tools

The EAP is completely confidential. Usage is never reported to the company. The helpline number is 0800 100 6789, available 365 days a year.


5. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

OptiAgent Solutions Ltd is committed to the continuous development of all employees.

Training Budget: Each employee receives an annual allowance of £2,000 to spend on external training courses, professional certifications, conferences, and relevant books or learning materials. Requests must be approved by the line manager and submitted through the L&D portal before booking. The budget resets on 1 January each year and cannot be carried forward.

Study Leave: Employees undertaking formal qualifications or professional examinations are entitled to 5 days of paid study leave per year. Study leave is in addition to annual leave and must be approved by the line manager in advance. Evidence of enrolment may be requested.


6. GYM MEMBERSHIP SUBSIDY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The company subsidises gym membership at partner gym networks (including PureGym, Nuffield Health, and Anytime Fitness) by up to £50 per month. The subsidy is paid directly to the gym provider. To activate this benefit, select your preferred gym through the benefits portal. Employees joining non-partner gyms may claim up to £50/month reimbursement upon submission of a monthly membership receipt.


7. CYCLE TO WORK SCHEME
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Under the government's Cycle to Work scheme, employees may obtain a bicycle and cycling equipment up to a value of £1,000 tax-free through a salary sacrifice arrangement. Repayments are spread over 12 months via payroll deduction. This scheme is available once every 4 years. Eligible items include bicycles, helmets, lights, locks, and waterproof clothing.


8. SEASON TICKET LOAN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Employees who commute by public transport may apply for an interest-free season ticket loan of up to £6,000 to cover the cost of an annual travel pass. The loan is repaid through equal monthly salary deductions over 10 months. Applications are reviewed at the start of each quarter. If employment ends before the loan is repaid, the outstanding balance will be deducted from the employee's final salary payment.


9. ANNUAL BONUS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Employees are eligible for a performance-based annual bonus of between 0% and 15% of their base salary. The bonus is calculated based on individual performance ratings, team performance, and overall company financial results for the preceding year. Bonus payments are made in March each year for the prior performance year (January to December).

Employees must be employed on the date of payment to receive a bonus. Employees who join mid-year are eligible for a pro-rata bonus in their first year. There is no guaranteed bonus element — all bonus payments are discretionary.


10. SHARE OPTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

After completing one year of continuous service, all employees are eligible to participate in the company's Enterprise Management Incentive (EMI) share option scheme. Options are granted annually and vest over a three-year period (one-third per year). Full details of the scheme rules, current option pricing, and vesting schedules are provided in the Share Options Welcome Pack issued by the Finance team following eligibility.
