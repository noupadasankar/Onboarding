OPTIAGENT SOLUTIONS LTD
PERFORMANCE REVIEW POLICY
Version 1.4 | Effective Date: 1 January 2026

1. PURPOSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This policy sets out OptiAgent Solutions Ltd's approach to performance management. Our performance review process is designed to support employee growth, align individual contribution with business objectives, and ensure that reward and recognition reflect genuine performance. All permanent employees are subject to this policy from the date of completing their probationary period.


2. ANNUAL REVIEW CYCLE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The performance year runs from 1 January to 31 December. There are two formal touchpoints within each cycle:

  Mid-Year Check-In (June): A structured conversation between the employee and their line manager to review progress against goals set in January, identify any support needs, and adjust priorities for the second half of the year. The check-in is documented in the HR system but does not result in a formal rating.

  Year-End Review (December): A full performance review conducted in December covering the entire calendar year. This review results in a formal rating and informs salary review and bonus decisions effective from 1 April the following year.


3. RATING SCALE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

All employees receive one of four performance ratings at year-end:

  Exceeds Expectations   — Consistently surpasses objectives; demonstrates leadership and impact beyond role scope.
  Meets Expectations     — Delivers on all objectives to the required standard; demonstrates the expected behaviours for the role.
  Needs Improvement      — Partially meets objectives; specific areas of development have been identified and must be addressed.
  Unsatisfactory         — Fails to meet key objectives or behavioural standards; formal intervention is required.

Ratings are not normalised to a forced distribution, though calibration is used to ensure consistency across teams.


4. REVIEW PROCESS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The year-end review follows a structured five-step process:

  Step 1 — Self-Assessment: The employee completes a self-assessment in the HR portal by 30 November, reflecting on progress against SMART goals and overall contribution.
  Step 2 — Manager Assessment: The line manager completes an independent assessment and draft rating by 7 December.
  Step 3 — Calibration: Line managers across the same business unit convene in a calibration session (week of 10 December) to ensure ratings are applied consistently and fairly across the team.
  Step 4 — Feedback Meeting: The line manager holds a one-to-one feedback meeting with the employee by 20 December to share the confirmed rating and discuss key themes.
  Step 5 — Sign-Off: The employee acknowledges the review in the HR portal. Employees may add a response comment. Sign-off is required by 31 December.


5. SMART GOALS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Performance goals must be Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound (SMART). Goals are set collaboratively between the employee and their line manager in January each year and logged in the HR system. Each employee should have between 4 and 6 goals. Goals are reviewed and updated at each quarterly check-in (March, June, September, December) to reflect any changes in business priorities.


6. SALARY REVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Salary increases are directly linked to year-end performance ratings and take effect on 1 April each year. The indicative salary increase ranges by rating are:

  Exceeds Expectations:   5% to 8% increase
  Meets Expectations:     2% to 4% increase
  Needs Improvement:      0% (no increase)
  Unsatisfactory:         0% (no increase; subject to PIP)

Final salary decisions are made by the Head of People & Culture in conjunction with Finance and approved by the relevant Director. Market benchmarking data is reviewed annually to ensure compensation remains competitive.


7. PROMOTION CRITERIA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Employees wishing to be considered for promotion to the next grade must meet all of the following criteria:

  - Achieved an Exceeds Expectations rating in two consecutive year-end reviews
  - Received a formal written endorsement from their line manager
  - Demonstrated the competencies and scope of responsibility required at the target grade (as defined in the role framework)

Promotions are reviewed during the annual calibration process and communicated alongside year-end review outcomes. There is no quota on the number of promotions granted in a given year; decisions are based entirely on demonstrated performance.


8. PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLAN (PIP)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

An Unsatisfactory year-end rating will automatically trigger a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). The PIP is a structured 90-day programme designed to give the employee a clear and supported opportunity to meet the required performance standard.

The PIP document will include: specific performance gaps, clearly defined success criteria, resources and support to be provided by the company, and a timeline with milestones. Weekly check-ins between the employee and line manager are mandatory throughout the PIP period, with written notes recorded after each meeting.

At the conclusion of the 90-day period, one of three outcomes will be confirmed: (1) successful completion and return to standard performance management; (2) extension of the PIP for a further period where partial improvement has been made; or (3) escalation to the disciplinary process if no meaningful improvement has occurred.

Employees on a PIP are not eligible for salary increases or bonus payments during the plan period.
