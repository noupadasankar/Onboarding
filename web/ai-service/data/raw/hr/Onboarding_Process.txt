1. INTRODUCTION & PURPOSE
================================================================================

This document is the official Onboarding Process Guide for OptiAgent Technologies. It applies to all new employees globally including full-time hires, fixed-term contract employees, and employees transferring into OptiAgent from an acquisition.

The goal of this guide is to ensure that every new OptiAgent employee has a consistent, high-quality onboarding experience that:

  - Makes them feel welcomed and valued from day one
  - Equips them with the tools, access, and knowledge needed to be effective
  - Familiarizes them with OptiAgent's culture, values, and ways of working
  - Sets clear expectations for the first 90 days
  - Ensures legal and compliance requirements are met promptly

This guide should be read in conjunction with the Employee Handbook (Document ID: HR-EH-001), which contains the company's policies on conduct, leave, benefits, performance, and more. Where this document and the Employee Handbook conflict, the Employee Handbook governs on matters of policy and this document governs on matters of onboarding process.

The People Operations team is responsible for coordinating the onboarding process. Your HRBP (HR Business Partner) is your primary HR contact throughout onboarding. To find your assigned HRBP, check Workday under My Profile > HR Contacts or email hr.global@optiagent.com.

2. PRE-JOINING ACTIVITIES (BEFORE DAY 1)
================================================================================

2.1 OFFER ACCEPTANCE & DOCUMENTATION
--------------------------------------

When you accept your offer, the Talent Operations team will send you a pre-joining checklist via email within 2 business days. This checklist includes:

  a) Signed offer letter (DocuSign)
  b) Proprietary Information and Invention Assignment Agreement (PIIA) — required before or on Day 1
  c) Employment eligibility verification documents (I-9 for USA; Right to Work check for UK; Form 16 data for India; etc.)
  d) Bank account details for payroll setup
  e) Emergency contact information
  f) Tax form (W-4 for USA; Starter Checklist for UK; Form 12BB for India)
  g) Background verification consent form
  h) Equipment preference form (Mac vs Windows; peripherals)

Deadline: All documentation must be submitted via the Onboarding Portal (onboarding.optiagent.com) at least 5 business days before your start date. Failure to submit documents on time may delay your system access setup or your first payroll.

Contact for documentation queries: talent.ops@optiagent.com

2.2 BACKGROUND VERIFICATION
------------------------------

All new hires are subject to a background verification process conducted by OptiAgent's third-party provider (HireRight). The background check covers:

  - Identity verification
  - Employment history (previous 5 years or last 3 employers, whichever is longer)
  - Educational qualification verification
  - Criminal record check (where legally permitted in your country)
  - Credit check (Finance roles, M3+ roles, and roles with access to financial systems only)
  - Global sanctions and watchlist screening

TIMELINE: Background checks typically take 5–10 business days. Your start date may be conditional on satisfactory completion of the background check. OptiAgent will notify you if the check reveals discrepancies or requires additional documentation.

INDIA-SPECIFIC: Address verification is also conducted for India hires.

GERMANY-SPECIFIC: Criminal background checks in Germany require explicit written consent and are restricted to information relevant to the specific role. The Betriebsrat (Works Council) is notified of the general background check practice per co-determination rules but not of individual results.

UK-SPECIFIC: Right to Work verification is mandatory before employment commences per the Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006. You will be required to present original right to work documents to your local HR team on or before Day 1.

If you have concerns about any part of the background check process, contact talent.ops@optiagent.com in confidence.

2.3 EQUIPMENT ALLOCATION REQUEST
-----------------------------------

IT Operations will contact you within 48 hours of your signed offer letter to confirm your equipment setup. Standard equipment:

  - Laptop (14-inch MacBook Pro or Dell Latitude/ThinkPad, based on role and preference)
  - Mouse and keyboard (wireless preferred)
  - Headset (Jabra or equivalent)
  - Monitor (for office-based roles, available at your desk; for remote roles, one external monitor is provided as part of the remote equipment package)

Engineers may request a second monitor; submit this request via the Equipment Preference Form in the Onboarding Portal. All requests above the standard kit require manager approval.

REMOTE HIRES: Equipment will be shipped to your home address 3–5 business days before your start date via tracked courier. You will receive a tracking number by email. If equipment does not arrive 2 days before your start date, contact it.help@optiagent.com immediately.

OFFICE HIRES: Equipment will be ready at the IT helpdesk or your desk on Day 1. Collect it as part of the Day 1 check-in process described in Section 3.

All company equipment remains the property of OptiAgent Technologies. You are responsible for the physical security and reasonable care of all equipment in your possession. Equipment policy details are in the Employee Handbook (HR-EH-001) Section 7 and Company Policy (OAT-CP-001) Section 3.3.

2.4 EMAIL & SYSTEM ACCOUNT CREATION
--------------------------------------

Your company email address (@optiagent.com) and core system accounts will be created by IT Operations 2 business days before your start date. The naming convention is:

  firstname.lastname@optiagent.com

If there is a name collision (another employee with the same first and last name), IT will add a middle initial or number: e.g., james.e.smith@optiagent.com.

You will receive your temporary Okta password via SMS on the personal mobile number provided in the Onboarding Portal. On Day 1, you must:

  a) Log in to login.optiagent.com (Okta)
  b) Change your password (must meet the requirements in Company Policy Section 4.1: minimum 14 characters, upper + lower + number + special character)
  c) Enroll your MFA device (Microsoft Authenticator is recommended; see Company Policy Section 4.2)
  d) Update your 1Password account (company-licensed password manager)

If you do not receive your Okta credentials by 8:00 AM on your start date, contact it.help@optiagent.com or call the IT helpdesk number in the Employee Handbook Section 5 (Internal Contacts).

Systems provisioned on Day 1:

  - Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive for Business)
  - Workday (HRIS)
  - ServiceNow (IT Helpdesk)
  - Confluence (internal wiki)
  - Jira or GitHub (Engineering roles)
  - Salesforce (Sales and Customer Success roles)
  - Slack (all employees)
  - Zoom (all employees)
  - L&D Portal (all employees)

Role-specific access to additional systems (production environments, customer databases, financial systems) is provisioned after Day 1 based on manager-submitted access requests. These follow a least-privilege access model; you will only be given access to systems you actively need for your role.

2.5 PRE-JOINING COMMUNICATION
--------------------------------

You will receive the following pre-joining communications:

  - Welcome email from your HRBP (immediately after offer acceptance)
  - Equipment confirmation email from IT Operations
  - Background verification invitation from HireRight
  - Pre-boarding portal access link (onboarding.optiagent.com)
  - "Welcome to OptiAgent" email from the CEO (sent 2 days before your start date)
  - Day 1 agenda from your manager

It is recommended that you review the public-facing OptiAgent website (optiagent.com), the Engineering Blog (blog.optiagent.com), and the public Glassdoor profile to familiarize yourself with the company's products, culture, and recent announcements before Day 1.

3. DAY 1: ARRIVAL & ORIENTATION
================================================================================

3.1 FIRST-DAY SCHEDULE OVERVIEW
----------------------------------

Your Day 1 will be coordinated by your HRBP and manager. A typical Day 1 schedule for office-based employees:

  09:00 AM  Arrival at office; reception check-in; access badge issuance
  09:15 AM  Equipment collection from IT helpdesk
  09:30 AM  IT Orientation (1–2 hours): system setup, Okta, MFA, VPN, email
  11:00 AM  HR Orientation (1 hour): company overview, policies, benefits briefing
  12:00 PM  Welcome lunch with your manager and immediate team
  01:00 PM  Workday self-service walkthrough (30 minutes)
  01:30 PM  Introduction to your team (team standup or virtual intro for hybrid teams)
  02:30 PM  Independent time: explore SharePoint, Confluence, Okta app catalog, and pre-assigned onboarding tasks in the L&D Portal
  05:00 PM  Optional: End-of-day check-in with your manager

For remote hires, the schedule above is replicated via Microsoft Teams and Zoom. Your equipment will have been delivered beforehand. Day 1 starts with an IT session via Teams to verify equipment setup before any other sessions.

3.2 OFFICE CHECK-IN AND ACCESS BADGE
---------------------------------------

Report to the reception desk of your office building. Bring a government-issued photo ID (passport, national ID card, or driving license). Facilities will issue you a temporary access card for Day 1; your permanent proximity access card will be ready within 3 business days.

Access card rules (from Company Policy OAT-CP-001 Section 11.1):

  - Your access card is personal and non-transferable
  - Do not allow others to tailgate through secure access points using your card
  - Report a lost or stolen card immediately to Facilities and IT Security
  - Sharing your access card is a terminable offense

Your access card grants access to: common areas, your primary office floor, shared meeting rooms, and the kitchen/amenity areas on your floor. Access to secure server rooms, data centers, and executive floors requires additional approval submitted by your manager via ServiceNow.

3.3 EQUIPMENT COLLECTION
--------------------------

Visit the IT helpdesk on Day 1 (location varies by office — shown on your Day 1 agenda). Bring your Okta temporary password and your government ID. IT will:

  a) Issue your laptop (pre-imaged with company software)
  b) Provide your mouse, keyboard, and headset
  c) Walk you through the first-login setup (covered in the IT Orientation)
  d) Register your devices in Microsoft Intune and the IT Asset Management system (ServiceNow)
  e) Issue your YubiKey hardware token if your role requires production system access

Sign the Equipment Receipt Form in Workday (pre-populated by IT). This confirms you have received the equipment and accept responsibility for it.

3.4 IT ORIENTATION SESSION
-----------------------------

The IT Orientation is a 1–2 hour session delivered by a member of the IT Helpdesk team (in-person or via Teams for remote hires). It covers:

  a) Okta login and MFA setup (Microsoft Authenticator app required on your personal phone)
  b) 1Password setup and password manager basics
  c) Microsoft 365 overview: Outlook configuration, Teams setup, SharePoint navigation
  d) GlobalProtect VPN installation and first connection
  e) Microsoft Intune enrollment for your device and any personal devices (BYOD)
  f) Security expectations: screen lock, clean desk, phishing awareness
  g) IT helpdesk access: ServiceNow portal (it.optiagent.com/helpdesk) and email (it.help@optiagent.com)

After the IT Orientation you should be able to:

  - Log in to all provisioned applications via Okta
  - Send and receive email in Outlook
  - Join meetings in Teams and Zoom
  - Connect to the VPN from off-network locations
  - Submit IT helpdesk tickets via ServiceNow

If any system is not accessible after the IT Orientation, raise a ServiceNow ticket immediately. Do not wait until Day 2.

3.5 HR ORIENTATION SESSION
-----------------------------

The HR Orientation is a 1-hour session delivered by your HRBP (in-person or via Teams). It covers:

  a) Welcome and introductions
  b) Company overview: mission, vision, and values (see Employee Handbook Section 1)
  c) Key HR policies: leave, attendance, performance, code of conduct
  d) Benefits overview: health insurance, retirement, EAP, wellness
  e) Workday self-service walkthrough: leave requests, payslips, expense claims
  f) Mandatory training requirements and deadlines
  g) How to reach HR: HRBP contacts, hr.global@optiagent.com, and the HR portal on SharePoint

You will receive a printed (or PDF) copy of:

  - Employee Handbook (HR-EH-001)
  - This Onboarding Process Guide (HR-OP-001)
  - Leave Policy (HR-LP-001)
  - Company Policy (OAT-CP-001)

These documents are also available on the HR SharePoint site, accessible via Okta > SharePoint > HR Documents.

3.6 BENEFITS ENROLLMENT BRIEFING
-----------------------------------

Your HRBP or a Benefits Specialist will walk you through benefits enrollment options during or shortly after the HR Orientation. Key points:

  - You have 30 calendar days from your start date to make benefits elections in Workday
  - Missing the 30-day window means you cannot change elections until Open Enrollment (October–November) or a qualifying life event
  - Benefits vary by country — see Employee Handbook Section 6 for your country's benefits overview
  - For USA: Medical, Dental, Vision (Aetna), 401(k) (Fidelity), FSA/HSA, Commuter Benefit, Life Insurance, STD/LTD
  - For UK: BUPA Private Medical, Aviva Pension, Cycle to Work, Life Assurance
  - For India: Star Health Group Medical, EPF/VPF, Gratuity Trust, LTA, HRA
  - For Germany: GKV contribution, company pension supplement, Life Insurance
  - For Singapore: Great Eastern Group Hospitalization, CPF, AWS (13th month)

Questions on benefits after the briefing should be directed to benefits@optiagent.com.

4. WEEK 1: FOUNDATION BUILDING
================================================================================

4.1 SECURITY & COMPLIANCE TRAINING
--------------------------------------

All new employees must complete the following mandatory training modules within 14 calendar days of their start date. Training is in the L&D Portal (accessible via Okta):

  MODULE 1: Security Awareness Basics (45 minutes)
    - Password hygiene, MFA, physical security, clean desk
    - PASS MARK: 80%

  MODULE 2: Phishing Awareness (30 minutes)
    - Recognizing phishing emails, reporting procedures
    - Includes a simulated phishing test within 30 days of hire
    - PASS MARK: 80%

  MODULE 3: Data Privacy Essentials (45 minutes)
    - GDPR basics, data classification, handling Restricted data
    - PASS MARK: 80%

  MODULE 4: Code of Conduct (30 minutes)
    - Ethics, conflict of interest, anti-harassment, whistleblowing
    - PASS MARK: 100% (attestation required)

  MODULE 5: AI Responsible Use (30 minutes)
    - Approved AI tools, prohibited uses, prompt hygiene
    - PASS MARK: 80%

Failure to complete these modules within 14 days will result in escalation to your manager. System access may be suspended for non-compliant employees after 21 days. The compliance training deadline for all annual renewals is December 31 each year.

4.2 PAYROLL SETUP
-------------------

To ensure your first paycheck is processed correctly:

  a) Verify your bank account details are entered in Workday (Pay > Payment Elections) within your first 3 business days
  b) Submit your completed tax forms (W-4 for USA; Starter Checklist for UK; Form 12BB for India; equivalent for Germany and Singapore) to payroll@optiagent.com within your first week
  c) Confirm your payroll details with your HRBP at your Day 1 or first-week check-in

PAYROLL CUTOFFS (entries must be in the system before these dates to appear on the next pay run):
  - USA: 5 business days before the 1st and 15th
  - UK, Germany, Singapore: 10th of the month
  - India: 10th of the month

If you start after the cutoff date for a given pay cycle your first paycheck will be in the following cycle. Any days worked in the gap period will be paid as a catch-up in the first paycheck. Contact payroll@optiagent.com with any payroll queries.

4.3 TEAM INTRODUCTION
-----------------------

By the end of Week 1 you should have been introduced to:

  - Your direct manager (formal 1:1 scheduled in the first 2 days)
  - Your immediate team (via standup, team meeting, or individual introductions)
  - Your HRBP
  - Your IT Helpdesk contact
  - Key cross-functional stakeholders (as determined by your manager)

Your manager is responsible for scheduling these introductions. If any of these have not happened by Day 5, raise it with your HRBP.

4.4 MANAGER INTRODUCTION & ALIGNMENT
---------------------------------------

Your manager will schedule a dedicated 1:1 in your first 2 days to discuss:

  a) Your role and immediate priorities
  b) Team structure and working norms
  c) Communication preferences (async vs sync; preferred tools)
  d) How success will be measured in your first 30/60/90 days
  e) Your initial 30-day plan (see Section 5)
  f) Any immediate questions you have about the role, team, or company

It is recommended that you prepare for this meeting by reviewing any job description, pre-reading materials, or team Confluence pages shared by your manager before Day 1.

4.5 SYSTEMS ACCESS VERIFICATION
----------------------------------

By end of Week 1, verify you have access to the following:

  [ ] Okta dashboard (login.optiagent.com)
  [ ] Company email (Outlook)
  [ ] Microsoft Teams
  [ ] SharePoint (HR Documents + team space)
  [ ] Workday (HRIS — leave, pay, benefits)
  [ ] ServiceNow (IT Helpdesk)
  [ ] Confluence (company wiki)
  [ ] L&D Portal (learning)
  [ ] Slack (team messaging)
  [ ] Role-specific tools (Jira/GitHub/Salesforce/etc.)
  [ ] VPN (GlobalProtect)

Raise a ServiceNow ticket for any missing access. If a system critical to your role is unavailable by Day 5, escalate to your manager and HRBP.

5. 30-DAY PLAN
================================================================================

The 30-day plan is focused on orientation, learning, and relationship building. You should not be expected to deliver major outputs in the first 30 days — the goal is to understand your environment deeply.

GOALS FOR DAY 30:

  UNDERSTANDING THE ROLE
    - Understand your team's mission and how it contributes to OptiAgent's overall strategy
    - Understand the key products, platforms, or processes you will be working on
    - Complete a review of existing team documentation, roadmaps, and project states
    - Identify the 3–5 most important stakeholders for your role and schedule introductions with them

  UNDERSTANDING THE COMPANY
    - Complete all mandatory onboarding training (Security, Code of Conduct, Data Privacy, AI Use, Phishing)
    - Read the Employee Handbook (HR-EH-001), Leave Policy (HR-LP-001), and Company Policy (OAT-CP-001)
    - Understand OptiAgent's mission, vision, values, and current business priorities (ask your manager for the most recent company all-hands deck)
    - Attend at least 1 company-wide or function-wide meeting (All Hands, department standup, town hall)

  TOOLS & SYSTEMS
    - Be fully onboarded into all required systems (checklist in Section 4.5)
    - Have submitted and had approved your benefits elections in Workday
    - Have set up your emergency contacts in Workday
    - Have confirmed your first paycheck was processed correctly

  RELATIONSHIP BUILDING
    - Complete 1:1 meetings with at least 5 key colleagues or stakeholders
    - Attend all relevant team meetings and working groups
    - Connect on LinkedIn with your immediate team (optional but recommended)

  FIRST DELIVERABLE
    - Produce a "first 30 days observations" note for your manager: a brief (1–2 page) document capturing your initial impressions, questions, and any early observations about where you can add value. This is not a performance evaluation but a structured reflection tool.

MILESTONE CHECK: Your manager will conduct an informal 30-day check-in to discuss progress on the above goals, answer questions, and adjust priorities if needed.

6. 60-DAY PLAN
================================================================================

By Day 60 you should be moving from learning to contributing. You should have enough context to start producing meaningful work and identifying where you can make an impact.

GOALS FOR DAY 60:

  CONTRIBUTING
    - Be actively contributing to at least one live project or workstream
    - Have taken ownership of at least one clearly defined task or deliverable
    - Start demonstrating the behaviors and practices expected at your level (see Salary Grades HR-SG-001 for level expectations)

  DEEPENING CONTEXT
    - Have had 1:1s with all key stakeholders relevant to your role
    - Understand your team's OKRs or KPIs and how your work connects to them
    - Have attended cross-functional meetings to understand how your team interfaces with Engineering, Sales, Product, Finance, or other departments (as relevant)

  PROCESS ADHERENCE
    - Be fully integrated into your team's development/delivery process (sprint ceremonies, code reviews, deal reviews, etc.)
    - Be raising questions and flagging risks proactively in appropriate channels
    - Have submitted at least one expense claim or leave request via Workday if applicable (to confirm you are familiar with self-service tools)

  PERFORMANCE DIALOGUE
    - Have a preliminary conversation with your manager about how performance will be measured in your role
    - Understand the performance rating framework (see Employee Handbook Section 13 and Salary Grades HR-SG-001 Promotion Matrix)
    - Identify 2–3 development areas and discuss learning resources with your manager (see learning budget in Employee Handbook Section 15 and Salary Grades HR-SG-001 Sheet 5)

MILESTONE CHECK: Your manager will conduct an informal 60-day check-in to discuss progress. If there are any performance concerns at Day 60 they should be raised clearly and in writing with HRBP involvement. Do not wait until Day 90.

================================================================================
7. 90-DAY PLAN
================================================================================

Day 90 is the end of your probation period. By this point you should be operating independently, delivering outcomes, and building a reputation as a trusted colleague.

GOALS FOR DAY 90:

  DELIVERING
    - Have completed at least one significant deliverable or project milestone
    - Be producing work that meets or exceeds expectations for your level
    - Be operating with minimal hand-holding on day-to-day tasks

  OPERATING INDEPENDENTLY
    - Make decisions within your scope without needing constant validation
    - Proactively manage your own schedule, priorities, and workload
    - Demonstrate good judgment in escalating vs. handling issues autonomously

  TEAM INTEGRATION
    - Be a recognized contributor to your team's culture and output
    - Have built positive working relationships with key colleagues and stakeholders
    - Understand the informal as well as formal organizational dynamics of your team

  PLANNING AHEAD
    - Have a draft of your H1 or H2 performance objectives agreed with your manager (depending on the time of year you joined)
    - Understand what "exceeds expectations" looks like in your role
    - Have identified and enrolled in at least one learning or development activity

MILESTONE CHECK: The formal 90-day probation review will result in one of three outcomes: Confirmed, Extended, or Released. See Section 8 for details.

8. PROBATION PERIOD & CONFIRMATION
================================================================================

8.1 PROBATION OVERVIEW
------------------------

All new OptiAgent employees serve a 3-month (90-day) probation period, regardless of seniority. The purpose of probation is to allow both the employee and OptiAgent to confirm that the role is a good fit in terms of performance, culture, and expectations.

During probation:
  - You are entitled to the same salary, leave, and benefits as a confirmed employee
  - Your employment can be terminated with shorter notice per the applicable country law (see Employee Handbook Section 20.2 for notice during probation by country)
  - You are not eligible for stock option grants until your probation is confirmed
  - You are eligible for the employee referral program from Day 1

8.2 30-DAY INFORMAL CHECK-IN
------------------------------

Your manager will meet with you informally at Day 30 to discuss:
  - How you are settling in
  - Whether you have access to everything you need
  - Early performance observations
  - Any concerns from either party

This meeting is not formally documented unless the manager identifies performance concerns, in which case a note is added to Workday.

8.3 60-DAY INFORMAL CHECK-IN
------------------------------

Your manager meets with you at Day 60 to discuss:
  - Progress against the 60-day goals
  - Whether you are on track for a smooth confirmation at Day 90
  - Any development actions needed before Day 90
  - Early identification of any performance gaps

If performance concerns arise at Day 60, your manager must inform the HRBP. The HRBP will determine whether a formal performance support plan should be initiated before the Day 90 review.

8.4 90-DAY FORMAL PROBATION REVIEW
-------------------------------------

The formal probation review is conducted by your manager with HRBP oversight. It results in one of three outcomes (Section 8.5, 8.6, or 8.7).

The review covers:
  - Performance against the 30/60/90-day plan
  - Core competencies for your role and level
  - Culture and values alignment
  - Feedback from key stakeholders (gathered informally by your manager)
  - Any concerns raised during the probation period

The review should be scheduled at least 5 business days before Day 90 so that the outcome can be communicated on or before the probation end date. You will be asked to complete a self-assessment in Workday before the review meeting.

8.5 CONFIRMATION LETTER
-------------------------

If your probation review is successful, your manager will issue a Confirmation Letter via Workday within 2 business days of the review meeting. The letter confirms:
  - Your employment status as confirmed
  - Your role, grade, and compensation
  - Your first formal performance review cycle (H1 or H2 depending on joining date)

Stock option grants (for eligible grades — see Salary Grades HR-SG-001 Sheet 4) are processed after confirmation. The grant will be reflected in your Workday profile within 30 days.

8.6 PROBATION EXTENSION
-------------------------

If performance during probation is below expectations but the manager and HRBP believe that additional time and support could result in a successful outcome, probation may be extended by up to 3 months (maximum total probation: 6 months).

Requirements for extension:
  - Written notice to the employee before the end of the original 90-day period
  - HRBP sign-off
  - Clear documented performance expectations for the extension period
  - Mid-point check-in during the extension period

Only one extension is permitted. If performance remains below expectations at the end of the extension period, the outcome is Release (Section 8.7).

8.7 PROBATION OUTCOME: RELEASE
--------------------------------

If performance during probation is not satisfactory and cannot be remediated, OptiAgent may end the employment at the end of the probation period. This decision:

  - Requires HRBP approval and Legal review
  - Must be communicated to the employee in writing before the end of the probation period
  - Includes payment of all salary and accrued leave up to the last working day
  - Does not typically include severance (country law governs; some jurisdictions require minimum notice even during probation)

The employee has the right to raise a grievance in accordance with the process in the Employee Handbook Section 3.7.

10. REMOTE EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING
================================================================================

Remote employees follow the same process as office-based employees with the following adaptations:

EQUIPMENT: Shipped to your home address 3–5 business days before start date. If equipment does not arrive in time, contact it.help@optiagent.com. You may use your personal device temporarily (subject to BYOD policy in Company Policy Section 5) but all sessions must still begin on Day 1.

DAY 1 SCHEDULE: Delivered entirely via Microsoft Teams. Your HRBP will share a virtual Day 1 agenda. All orientation sessions (IT, HR, Benefits) are conducted via Teams calls. Ensure your personal internet connection is stable before Day 1; if you have connectivity concerns, contact it.help@optiagent.com for troubleshooting assistance.

EQUIPMENT RETURNS: For remote employees, return of equipment at end of employment is via pre-paid courier. IT will arrange collection within 5 business days of your last day. Failure to return equipment may result in deduction from final settlement.

INTERNET ALLOWANCE: Designated remote employees receive a monthly internet allowance of USD 60/month (or country equivalent) paid via Workday Expense > Internet Allowance. This begins from your start date. See Salary Grades (HR-SG-001) Sheet 5 for country-specific amounts.

HOME OFFICE SETUP: A one-time home office allowance of USD 500 (or country equivalent) is available for remote employees for desk, chair, and peripheral expenses. Submit via Workday Expense with receipts within 90 days of your start date.

VIRTUAL SOCIAL INTEGRATION: Your manager is encouraged to proactively create virtual social touchpoints (coffee chats, team lunches via Uber Eats credit, virtual team events) to help remote employees build relationships. If you feel isolated or disconnected, raise it with your manager or HRBP — this is a known risk for remote hires and OptiAgent takes it seriously.

11. INTERNATIONAL HIRE ONBOARDING CONSIDERATIONS
================================================================================

For employees joining in a country other than their country of origin (international hires, relocations), the following additional steps apply:

IMMIGRATION & RIGHT TO WORK:
  - Work authorization (visa, work permit) must be in place before the start date
  - OptiAgent's immigration provider (Fragomen) will coordinate work permit applications for sponsored hires
  - Contact mobility@optiagent.com for any immigration-related questions
  - Do not begin work without valid work authorization — this is a legal compliance requirement

RELOCATION ASSISTANCE:
  - Relocation assistance is provided per the Relocation Policy (see Employee Handbook Section 12.6)
  - IC1–IC3: USD 3,000 one-time; IC4+: USD 6,000 one-time; M3+: USD 10,000 with temporary accommodation
  - International relocations include 3 months of temporary accommodation support
  - Relocation payments are processed via Payroll after your start date

TAX IMPLICATIONS:
  - International assignees and relocated employees may have complex tax situations (dual tax residency, split-year treatment, tax equalization)
  - OptiAgent provides tax advisory support via our tax partner (Deloitte) for the first year of international assignment
  - Contact mobility@optiagent.com for tax support referrals

BANKING IN A NEW COUNTRY:
  - Setting up a local bank account may take 2–4 weeks. In the interim, payroll may be held or deposited to an existing account in another country where possible
  - Contact payroll@optiagent.com to discuss options

12. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
================================================================================

Q: What if my laptop doesn't arrive before Day 1?
A: Contact it.help@optiagent.com immediately. IT will provide a loaner device or arrange same-day delivery where possible. You may also use your personal device temporarily under the BYOD policy (Company Policy Section 5) for Day 1 sessions only.

Q: Can I start before all my background check results are in?
A: In most cases, conditional start dates are permitted if the majority of the background check is complete and there are no red flags. The final determination is made by Talent Ops on a case-by-case basis. Contact talent.ops@optiagent.com.

Q: What is the difference between annual leave and sick leave?
A: Annual leave is your planned vacation and personal time off, accrued throughout the year. Sick leave is for illness or medical reasons. They are tracked separately in Workday. See Leave Policy (HR-LP-001) Sections 3 and 4 for full details.

Q: How many annual leave days do I get?
A: This depends on your country and grade. India: 18 days; USA: 15 days (accrued monthly); UK: 25 days; Germany: 28 days; Singapore: 14–18 days. See Leave Policy (HR-LP-001) for full details by country.

Q: What is the holiday calendar for my office?
A: The full holiday calendar is in the Holiday Calendar document (HR-HC-001) available on the HR SharePoint site. You can also subscribe to the OptiAgent Holidays calendar in Outlook.

Q: When will I receive my first payslip?
A: Your first payslip will be in Workday (Pay > Payslips) on the pay date of your first complete pay cycle. If you joined after the payroll cutoff, you may receive a catch-up payment in the second cycle. Contact payroll@optiagent.com if you have concerns.

Q: Who sets my performance objectives?
A: Your manager sets your initial objectives in collaboration with you, typically in the first 30–60 days. Objectives are entered in Workday under Performance. See Employee Handbook Section 13 for the performance management process.

Q: What is my job grade / level?
A: Your grade (IC1–IC7 or M1–M5) is confirmed in your offer letter and visible in Workday under My Profile > Job. See Salary Grades (HR-SG-001) for the full level descriptions and what they mean for your compensation and career progression.

Q: Can I access my payslip before payday?
A: No. Payslips are generated on payday and are not available in advance. If you need proof of employment income before then, contact hr.global@optiagent.com to request an employment verification letter.

Q: What should I do if I have a concern about my manager or the workplace?
A: Speak to your HRBP. If your concern is about your HRBP, you can contact hr.global@optiagent.com directly or use the anonymous Ethics Hotline (1-800-OPT-ETHX or ethics.optiagent.com). See Employee Handbook Section 3 and Section 17.

Q: What training is mandatory in my first year?
A: In your first 14 days: Security Awareness, Phishing, Data Privacy, Code of Conduct, and AI Responsible Use. Annually by December 31: the same modules plus any role-specific compliance training. See Section 4.1 of this document.

Q: Is there a buddy or mentoring program for new employees?
A: Yes. The onboarding buddy program pairs you with an experienced colleague in a similar role for your first 90 days. Your buddy is assigned by your manager or HRBP within the first week. The MentorConnect program (Employee Handbook Section 15.5) is available after your probation is confirmed.

Q: Can I take leave during my probation period?
A: Yes. Annual leave and sick leave are available from Day 1. However, taking extended leave during probation may affect your ability to complete the 90-day plan objectives. Discuss with your manager and HRBP in advance.

Q: What happens to my unvested stock options if I resign before cliff?
A: All unvested options are forfeited. Vested options must be exercised within 90 days of your last day. See Salary Grades (HR-SG-001) Sheet 4 and your individual grant agreement. Contact equity@optiagent.com for your specific situation.

Q: Where can I find information on the company's AI usage policy?
A: Company Policy (OAT-CP-001) Section 12 covers the AI Usage Policy and Generative AI Guidelines. Approved tools include the OptiAgent Enterprise Platform, Microsoft 365 Copilot, GitHub Copilot (Engineering only), and Grammarly Business.
