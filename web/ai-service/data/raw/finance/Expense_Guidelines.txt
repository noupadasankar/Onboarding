PART 1: SUBMITTING AN EXPENSE CLAIM — STEP BY STEP
================================================================================

All expenses must be submitted via Workday.
Path: Workday > Pay > Expense > Create Expense Report

STEP 1 — COLLECT YOUR RECEIPT
At the time of purchase, obtain a receipt. A valid receipt must show:
  • Vendor name and address (or logo)
  • Date of purchase
  • Items purchased or service description
  • Amount paid and currency
  • Payment method (optional but preferred)

Credit card statements are NOT accepted as receipts. You need the original
itemized receipt or invoice.

Photographs of paper receipts (taken with your phone) are accepted as long as
the image is clear, complete, and readable. Use the Workday mobile app to
photograph and attach receipts on the go.

STEP 2 — CREATE AN EXPENSE REPORT IN WORKDAY
Log in to Workday. Navigate to Pay > Expense > Create Expense Report.
  • Report Name: Use a descriptive name, e.g., "NYC Client Visit — March 2025"
  • Business Purpose: Required field — describe the business reason clearly
    (e.g., "Dinner with Accenture team to discuss Q2 renewal")
  • Cost Center: Select your cost center (visible in Workday > My Profile > Job)

STEP 3 — ADD EXPENSE LINE ITEMS
For each item:
  • Select the correct Expense Type from the dropdown
  • Enter the exact amount (in the currency paid; Workday converts to USD)
  • Enter the date of the expense
  • Attach the receipt
  • For meal expenses: list all attendees and their companies in the memo field

STEP 4 — SUBMIT FOR APPROVAL
Submit the expense report. Your manager receives an automated notification.
Most expense reports are approved within 3–5 business days.

STEP 5 — REIMBURSEMENT
After manager approval, Finance processes reimbursement. Payment is made:
  • USA: via ACH to your bank account on file — within 2 pay cycles (approx. 15 days)
  • UK, Germany, Singapore: added to monthly payroll
  • India: added to monthly payroll

DEADLINES:
  • Submit within 30 calendar days of the expense date
  • Claims submitted after 30 days require manager justification
  • Claims submitted after 90 days will generally be rejected (business reason
    and Finance Director approval required)

PART 3: PER DIEM RATES (MEALS & INCIDENTALS WHILE TRAVELLING)
================================================================================

Per diem is an alternative to itemized meal receipts during business travel.
You CANNOT mix per diem and itemized receipts for the same day.

COUNTRY          | STANDARD RATE / DAY | DIRECTOR+ RATE
-----------------|---------------------|----------------------
USA              | USD 75              | USD 100
UK               | GBP 60              | GBP 80
Germany          | EUR 28 (≥8 hrs)     | EUR 28 (legal max; cannot increase)
                 | EUR 14 (< 8 hrs)    |
India            | INR 2,000           | INR 3,000
Singapore        | SGD 80              | SGD 110

GERMANY NOTE: German per diem rates are set by law (Reisekostenrecht).
The deductible daily rate is EUR 28 for full days away from home (> 24 hrs)
and EUR 14 for 8–24 hrs. These are the maximum tax-free reimbursable amounts.
OptiAgent reimburses at the legal rate only.

USA NOTE: The IRS standard CONUS per diem rate varies by city. OptiAgent uses
the flat USD 75/day rate for simplicity, which covers most US locations.
High-cost cities (NYC, SF, DC, Boston) are eligible for up to USD 100/day with
manager approval and a note in the expense report.

INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL MEALS:
For countries not listed above, Finance will determine an appropriate rate
based on HMRC or State Department foreign per diem tables. Email
ap@optiagent.com before your trip to confirm the applicable rate.

PART 4: TRAVEL BOOKING RULES
================================================================================

ALL BUSINESS TRAVEL MUST BE BOOKED VIA EGENCIA.
Portal: travel.optiagent.com (via Okta SSO)

Booking outside Egencia is only permitted when:
  (a) The required hotel is not available in Egencia (e.g., conference hotel);
  (b) A significantly cheaper rate is available directly (> 20% savings);
      — Requires manager written approval BEFORE booking
  In either case, attach the justification note to your expense claim.

ADVANCE BOOKING DISCOUNTS:
  • Book domestic flights at least 7 days in advance
  • Book international flights at least 14 days in advance
  • Same-day and next-day bookings above USD 200 (domestic) or USD 800
    (international) require manager justification

FLIGHT CLASS REMINDER:
  • Economy: all employees, all flights (default)
  • Premium Economy: any employee for flights ≥ 3 hours (no additional approval)
  • Business Class: Director (M3) and above for flights ≥ 6 hours only
  • Business Class for IC-level: VP approval required + business justification
  • FIRST CLASS: NEVER PERMITTED for any employee

HOTEL RULES:
  • Book the cheapest available option within the city limit
  • Avoid choosing hotels more than 45 minutes from your meeting venue unless
    price difference justifies it
  • If all hotels are above the limit (e.g., during a major conference), document
    this and get manager approval before booking

LOYALTY POINTS:
  You may keep personal airline, hotel, and car rental loyalty points earned on
  company travel. However, you must not choose a more expensive option solely
  to earn points. Loyalty status must not influence travel decisions in a way
  that increases company costs.

CANCELLATIONS:
  Cancel bookings as early as possible to minimize cancellation fees. If you
  cancel a non-refundable booking without a valid business reason, the
  cancellation fee may be at your own cost. Always cancel via Egencia to
  ensure the charge is processed correctly.

PART 5: MEALS & ENTERTAINMENT
================================================================================

BUSINESS MEALS — KEY RULES:
1. The business purpose must be documented (what was discussed; what was decided)
2. All attendee names and their companies must be listed in the expense claim
3. The most senior OptiAgent employee present should pay and claim
4. Alcohol is permitted within the meal limit but excessive alcohol is not
   reimbursable and is a conduct issue. Use judgment.
5. Meals for internal team meetings are only reimbursable if:
   — The meeting is ≥ 4 hours long, OR
   — The meeting runs directly through a mealtime (e.g., 12:00–13:00)
   — Informal team lunches without a formal meeting agenda are not reimbursable

CLIENT ENTERTAINMENT ABOVE USD 100/PERSON:
Requires Director approval before the event. Submit a pre-approval email to your
manager at least 48 hours in advance. The pre-approval email must state:
  • Client name and company
  • Nature of the entertainment (dinner, sporting event, etc.)
  • Estimated total cost
  • Business purpose

SPORTING EVENTS / CONCERTS / THEATER:
Client entertainment at events (tickets) follows the same rules as meals.
Per-person limit is USD 150 with Director approval. Tickets above USD 150 per
person require VP approval. Always document the business purpose.

ALCOHOL AT INTERNAL EVENTS:
Bar tabs at internal company events (team off-sites, celebrations) are
reimbursable at up to USD 25/person (included in the per-person event limit).
Bar tabs above this require VP approval.

WORKING MEALS (DESK LUNCH / DELIVERY):
Meals ordered in to the office during a working lunch meeting are reimbursable
at up to USD 25/person. DoorDash, Uber Eats, or local equivalents are fine.
Attach the itemized order receipt (not just the payment confirmation).

PART 6: GIFTS & HOSPITALITY
================================================================================

CLIENT GIFTS:
  • Maximum: USD 75 per gift per recipient per year
  • All gifts require manager approval before purchase
  • Cash gifts, gift cards, or gift certificates to clients: NEVER permitted
  • Gifts must be branded (OptiAgent branded preferred) or generic professional
    items (wine, books, food hampers)
  • Gifts to government officials: ZERO TOLERANCE — never give gifts to
    government employees, regulators, or public officials without Legal approval

RECEIVING GIFTS FROM VENDORS/CLIENTS:
  • Under USD 50: may be accepted; no declaration needed
  • USD 50–150: must be declared in the Gift & Hospitality Register (Workday)
  • Above USD 150: must be declined OR donated to company common areas (e.g.,
    food items) and declared in the register
  • Cash gifts: NEVER accept, regardless of amount
  See Employee Handbook Section 16.3 for the full gift policy.

VENDOR-SPONSORED EVENTS:
If a vendor invites you to a conference, sporting event, or dinner:
  • Under USD 50 total value: accept without declaration
  • USD 50–150: declare in the Gift & Hospitality Register
  • Above USD 150: requires Manager + HRBP pre-approval before accepting
  • Vendor-sponsored international trips: always require VP + HRBP approval

PART 7: TECHNOLOGY & SOFTWARE EXPENSES
================================================================================

PERSONAL MOBILE PHONE BILL:
Claim your monthly phone bill via Workday Expense > Mobile Allowance.
Select your applicable limit based on your grade (see Part 2).
Attach your monthly bill or a screenshot showing the total amount.
You do NOT need to submit an itemized call log.

INTERNET ALLOWANCE (REMOTE EMPLOYEES):
If you are a designated full remote employee (specified in your offer letter or
a formal remote work agreement), you receive USD 60/month (or country equivalent)
as a monthly internet allowance. Submit via Workday Expense > Internet Allowance
each month with a copy of your bill or a brief note if your bill is on a flat
rate. Partial months are pro-rated.

HOME OFFICE EQUIPMENT (REMOTE EMPLOYEES):
You have a one-time USD 500 home office equipment allowance within the first
90 days of remote designation. Eligible items:
  • Desk or standing desk
  • Ergonomic chair
  • External monitor (if not provided by IT)
  • Keyboard, mouse, headset (if not provided by IT)
  • Webcam (if not provided by IT)
  • Cable management accessories

NOT ELIGIBLE: Decorative items, personal furniture not used for work,
printers (request via IT for a business printer if needed).

PERSONAL SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTIONS:
Personal software subscriptions (Adobe Creative Cloud personal, Grammarly
personal, etc.) are NOT reimbursable. If you need a business tool, request it
via ServiceNow (IT > Software Request). Standard tools are approved within
2 business days. Non-standard tools require IT Security review.

PART 8: COMMON EXPENSE MISTAKES (AND HOW TO AVOID THEM)
================================================================================

MISTAKE 1: No receipt for claims ≥ USD 25
FIX: Always get a receipt at point of purchase. Use your phone camera
immediately — do not rely on emailed receipts that might be missed.

MISTAKE 2: Credit card statement submitted instead of itemized receipt
FIX: Your card statement shows you paid but not WHAT you paid for. Get the
merchant receipt. If lost, request a duplicate from the vendor.

MISTAKE 3: Business purpose is too vague ("dinner" or "travel")
FIX: Be specific: "Dinner with Google Cloud partnership team to discuss
co-sell agreement for Q3 2025" is acceptable. "Dinner" is not.

MISTAKE 4: Claiming above your category limit without approval
FIX: Know your limits (Part 2). If you need to exceed a limit, get written
manager approval before incurring the expense and attach the approval email
to your claim.

MISTAKE 5: Claiming non-reimbursable items
FIX: Read Section 4.4 of Finance_Policy.docx. When in doubt, email
ap@optiagent.com before spending.

MISTAKE 6: Submitting after 30 days without a reason
FIX: Submit within 30 days. If you missed the window, explain why in the
"Business Purpose" field and get your manager's written acknowledgment.

MISTAKE 7: Missing attendee names for meal expenses
FIX: List every person present at a business meal: "Jane Smith (Google),
Raj Patel (OptiAgent AE), Alice Chen (OptiAgent CSM)."

MISTAKE 8: Booking travel outside Egencia without approval
FIX: Book via travel.optiagent.com. If you must book externally, get written
manager approval first and attach it to your expense claim.

MISTAKE 9: Corporate card used for personal expenses
FIX: Keep personal and business spending separate. If you accidentally charge
a personal item to your corporate card, repay it within 14 days via the
repayment process in Workday.

MISTAKE 10: Claiming mini-bar charges or personal in-flight entertainment
FIX: These are never reimbursable. See Section 4.4 of Finance Policy.
